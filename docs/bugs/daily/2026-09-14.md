# Bug Ledger Daily - 2026-09-14

## CL-148 — Bitget Classic hedge close wire side was rejected by history evidence

Status: **closed** (production-verified).

### Symptom

Since the `faadf563` deployment (2026-09-05), every Bitget-leg funding close that
fell back to taker flattening ended as an irrecoverable `no_candidate` audit debt.
At inspection time (2026-09-14) thirteen `pending_close_reconciliation` owners were
stuck, the production health gate failed with `pending_close_owner_present`, and the
eleven blocking rows suppressed conflicting new risk on ONGUSDT/CYSUSDT/LAUSDT/TUSDT.

Observed input/state: `entry-1789066343561-ONGUSDT` closed its Bitget long leg on
2026-09-10 19:00 UTC; the flat probe confirmed both venues at zero, and the billing
debt terminalized `automatic_history_no_candidate` within 33 seconds.

Observed output/state transition: `reconciliation.automatic_historical_evidence_blocked
classification=no_candidate` → `exit.billing_evidence_debt_irrecoverable` (operator
import required), with no further automatic attempts for days.

### Root cause

When a Bitget close is submitted in Classic hedge mode, the order builder inverts
the business close side on the wire (`_build_bitget_order_request`: `buy` +
`tradeSide=close` + `posSide=long` closes a long; `sell` + `close` + `posSide=short`
closes a short). Exchange order history therefore reports the open-side direction
for our close fills. `find_bitget_historical_close_order_candidates` compared that
raw wire side against the business close side (`close_order_side_for_position`),
so the real closing row was filtered out of unique-history discovery even when the
exchange held a complete, filled, fee-bearing row; discovery returned `no_candidate`
and the debt terminalized. The 2026-09-05 repair (CL-147) wired the discovery and
accepted empty pages but preserved this side predicate, and its closure evidence
treated the resulting `no_candidate` as the correct terminal outcome.

Exchange-truth evidence gathered read-only against `/api/v2/mix/order/orders-history`
and `/api/v2/mix/order/detail` for the traced position:

- close order `1482019161004191745` (`lfexf948f037b1454a27`): `side=buy`,
  `tradeSide=close`, `posSide=long`, `status=filled`, `baseVolume=303`,
  `priceAvg=0.07912` — a unique complete candidate that the filter rejected;
- the stored maker identity `1482019066493939713` is a genuine zero-fill canceled
  order (detail now `40109`), i.e. the debt's known-leg identity was correct but
  pointed at the wrong (unfilled) attempt.

### Contract

A Bitget history row closes a position side when venue semantics prove ownership
(`posSide` matching the position plus a close marker, or one-way `reduceOnly`) and
its wire side follows either documented hedge convention: the Classic builder's
repeated open side, or the UTA/one-way opposite business side. Order-status
reconciliation for a proven close row reports the business close side so billing
evidence and `CloseRuntime` completeness checks compare like for like. Open and
one-way rows are unaffected.

### Counterexample matrix

- Classic hedge close long (wire `buy`+`close`) and close short (wire `sell`+`close`)
  are candidates for their position sides (RED on the pre-fix tree).
- A hedge opening row (`tradeSide=open`) and a posSide-mismatched close row are
  rejected.
- A marker-less UTA hedge opening row (posSide=long, no `tradeSide`, open-side
  wire direction) must not borrow the Classic repeated-open-side convention;
  only explicitly close-marked hedge rows may (review follow-up `3be8d703`,
  RED on `5baee72`).
- A one-way row must carry the business close side; a `buy` reduce-only row is a
  SHORT-close candidate only.
- The parser normalizes only proven close rows: Classic marker or UTA
  opposite-side rows flip to the business side; `tradeSide=open` and one-way rows
  keep the wire side.
- Future legitimate misses record `bitget_history_no_candidate`; the one-shot
  reactivation covers only the legacy `no_candidate` reason with a Bitget or Aster
  leg, so a repaired miss can never reopen.

### Repair (`5baee72`, review follow-up `3be8d703`)

- `find_bitget_historical_close_order_candidates` accepts both hedge close wire
  conventions for `posSide` rows via `_bitget_close_wire_side_matches`; the
  repeated-open-side convention is restricted to explicitly close-marked rows
  (`3be8d703`) so marker-less UTA opening rows cannot become false candidates;
  ownership and quantity/time/status filters unchanged.
- `_parse_order_status_bitget` reports the business close side for `posSide` rows
  proven close-owned; all other rows keep the wire side.
- Discovery's empty-result classification is renamed `bitget_history_no_candidate`.
- `_reactivate_history_discovery_upgrade` extends the one-shot legacy
  `no_candidate` reactivation to debts with a Bitget leg (the Aster branch is
  unchanged and shares the legacy reason constant).
- Passive one-sided/excess IOC retries mint per-attempt client order ids
  (`exit_live_one_sided_*`/`exit_live_excess_*`): a truth-gap retry previously
  resent the accepted order's deterministic clientOid and Bitget rejected it with
  `40786 Duplicate clientOid` (traced on `entry-1789105961703-ONGUSDT`,
  2026-09-11 06:20 UTC). This is recorded separately as CL-149.
