### Production Symptom

The latest post-deploy audit exposed four failures that shared one semantic
root: trading risk, close accounting, lifecycle truth, and offline training
samples were not separated strongly enough.

- A recent LAB close was exchange-flat/no-open-orders and not a live-risk
  problem, but project records retained only `exit.closed` plus terminal-flat
  evidence instead of a complete `exit.reconciled` accounting statement.
- Bitget `40309 symbol has been removed` open-order probes for stale symbols
  could be treated like true open-order risk when no active local owner existed.
- Lifecycle truth could count the same close order from multiple sources twice,
  or let a close-query source turn an already known open order into close
  coverage.
- Offline PnL/report samples could be admitted with complete-looking numeric
  fields while funding/fee/price component references were incomplete.

### Root Cause

The system had multiple partially overlapping truth layers:

- Runtime close cleanup used exchange flatness to release trading risk, but the
  final accounting work could be dropped or archived instead of retained as
  nonblocking backfill.
- Diagnose treated an open-order probe error as equivalent to an open order,
  without first asking whether the symbol was stale/delisted and ownerless.
- The lifecycle ledger deduped by local event shape rather than a
  venue-normalized exchange identity and trusted source names too much for
  phase inference.
- Offline analysis required PnL fields, but not each component's exchange
  evidence refs; `funding_pnl_quote=0` without a funding statement could look
  complete.

### Unified Business Decision

- `exchange_bad` means real unexpected exchange position, real live open order,
  or missing required evidence for an active owner.
- `terminal_flat_accounting_gap` means exchange truth is flat and open orders
  are flat, so trading is not blocked, but close statements/backfill continue.
- `exchange_truth_probe_gap` means stale/delisted/unsupported ownerless symbol
  probe failure; it is diagnostic evidence, not live open-order truth.
- `exchange_lifecycle_complete` requires deduped open/close coverage at target
  quantity with no overcoverage gap and component evidence available.
- `phantom_zero_open` and samples with missing funding/fee/market evidence stay
  out of optimization training.

### Fix

- Runtime close reconciliation retains ACK-only/terminal-flat close identity
  history as `accounting_only_backfill=true`,
  `blocking_trading=false`, and
  `close_reconciliation_state=terminal_flat_accounting_gap`.
- Diagnose now emits `exchange_truth.probe_gaps`,
  `exchange_truth_actual_open_orders_present`,
  stale-symbol probe counts, and blocks only active-owner/current-candidate
  probe gaps.
- Lifecycle ledger dedupes fills by venue-normalized trade/exec/order identity,
  rejects weak close coverage that conflicts with an already known open order,
  records `raw_coverage`, `normalized_coverage`, `overcoverage_gaps`, and
  `component_evidence`. `tradeSide=open/close` now outranks generic source
  labels such as `exchange_trade_history`.
- Offline analysis propagates PnL component evidence, excludes
  `funding_statement_pending`, missing fee statement evidence, and
  overcoverage samples; the report runner's strict validator refuses to
  promote invalid `latest.*` artifacts, including old samples without a
  `component_evidence` object.
