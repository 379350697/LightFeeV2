# 2026-06-22

## Cluster CL-106 - Unified diagnostic visibility hardening after `92941a7`

| Status | Severity | Family | Modules | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| local semantic hardening | medium | diagnostic visibility, close artifact terminal truth, quote admission blockers, exchange catalog probe noise | business-contract, diagnose-live, bug-ledger | `lightfee/engine/business_contract.py`, `scripts/diagnose_live.py`, `tests/test_business_contract.py`, `tests/test_diagnose_live.py` |

### Fingerprint

`92941a7 cloud truth clean; resolved close artifacts; quote stale candidate blocks; unsupported-symbol probe noise; diagnostic_noise_summary category split`

### Summary

The latest production review after `92941a7` showed current trading truth was
clean: exchange flat, no open orders, no pending entry/close/residual repair,
and completed open/close lifecycles. The remaining risk was diagnostic drift:
resolved close artifacts, entry quote-admission blockers, and exchange catalog
probe noise could be read together as generic "problems" even though only one
of those classes can represent current live exposure.

This closure keeps trading behavior unchanged and hardens the shared business
vocabulary. `classify_noise_visibility(...)` is the single source for the
visibility split:

- `current_blocker`: current exposure, untrusted truth, unresolved close owner,
  or another condition that can block business terminality.
- `historical_terminal_evidence`: historical close/order artifacts after
  current exchange truth proves flat and no open orders.
- `current_admission_blocker`: candidate-level quote/OI/admission evidence that
  blocks new entry, but is not current exposure.
- `catalog_diagnostic`: unsupported-symbol probe records caused by venue symbol
  catalog scope, not by live position/open-order risk.

### Root Cause

- Close artifacts such as Binance `-2022`, Binance close post-only `-5022`,
  Bybit duplicate `110072`, ACK-only, and zero-fill are not terminal by
  themselves. They become historical only after exchange position and
  open-order truth are clean.
- Diagnose summary-derived close artifact counters must obey the same rule.
  Even if an upstream summary calls an artifact resolved, dirty or explicitly
  untrusted exchange truth keeps it in `current_blocker`.
- Quote stale and quote rewarm terminal stale belong to entry candidate
  admission. They should explain why a candidate was skipped, not appear as
  current exposure.
- `recovery.live_position_probe_unsupported_symbols` can be a venue-catalog
  filter artifact when mixed exchange universes are probed. It should remain
  visible for audit but must not affect the production gate.

### Fix

- `classify_noise_visibility(...)` now classifies unsupported-symbol probe
  records as `catalog_diagnostic` with scope
  `exchange_truth_catalog_filter`.
- Diagnose root `diagnostic_noise_summary` now exposes:
  `catalog_filtered_probe_count`, `admission_blocker_reason_counts`, and
  `historical_terminal_reason_counts`.
- `diagnostic_noise_summary` now gates summary-derived resolved close artifact
  counters behind current flat/no-open-orders truth; untrusted counters are
  reported as `unresolved_close_artifact` current blockers.
- Existing `current_blocker_count` and `current_admission_blocker_count` remain
  unchanged in meaning: admission blockers do not imply current exposure, and
  catalog diagnostics do not block the gate.

### Verification

- RED/GREEN coverage added in `tests/test_business_contract.py` for Bybit
  `110072`, Binance `-2022`, Binance close post-only `-5022`, zero-fill,
  quote stale/rewarm terminal stale, and unsupported-symbol catalog probes.
- RED/GREEN coverage added in `tests/test_diagnose_live.py` for a flat
  since-deploy-style mixed window where close artifacts are historical, quote
  stale records are admission blockers, unsupported-symbol probes are catalog
  diagnostics, and `current_blocker_count` remains zero.
- RED/GREEN coverage also verifies dirty exchange truth cannot historicalize
  summary-derived close artifacts; they become `current_blocker` instead.
