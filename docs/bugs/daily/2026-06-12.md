### Production Symptom

After the WS-BBO/Local-L2 production cutover, `live_tick_stale` recurred after
the initial warmup/transient had settled. Exchange truth still showed a flat
account with no open orders, but the live runtime reported `active_lane=full_tick`
over its 60s lane budget. The first deploy-window journal sample showed one scan
with 112 quote revalidate targets, 112 resolved quote-truth targets, and 58 REST
top-book resolutions. After the quote-scope fix was deployed as `26d4ff1`, the
post-deploy no-entry scan showed `tradeable_count=0`, all `quote_truth_*`
counters at `0`, and no quote fanout; nevertheless `live_tick_stale` recurred
again. `/proc` evidence showed the live process was CPU-bound (`Rsl`, about
96% CPU), not waiting on REST/socket I/O.
After the snapshot-observability follow-up was deployed as `13ea417`, quote
revalidate remained scoped down (`target_count=16`,
`quote_truth_rest_resolved_count=4`), but `live_tick_stale` recurred with
`active_lane_age_ms=66949` over the 60000ms budget and the live process again
CPU-bound. The journal showed many `runtime.snapshot_freshness_decision` records
for untracked candidates, proving the final freshness filter was still using the
full tradeable set.
After the final-filter fix was deployed as `a87c249`, `live_tick_stale` recurred
again during `last_good_sidecar` warmup. The deploy-window event stream showed
only `runtime.snapshot_fallback_last_good`,
`runtime.live_scan_revalidate_required`, and
`runtime.live_scan_recovery_warmup`, with no quote revalidate fanout yet, but
the fallback health payload was still built from a 4689-candidate snapshot and
the live process stayed CPU-bound.

### Root Cause

The earlier CL-071 fix correctly allowed WS-BBO budget-excluded top candidate
legs to fall back to REST top-book truth, but it left the revalidation scope at
the whole `tradeable` shortlist. That drifted from V1: V1 may keep a large scan
candidate pool, but execution-grade market data is assigned only to the primary
plus shadow tracked opportunities. In V2 WS-BBO mode, every stale quote leg in
the broad shortlist could therefore enter the serial synchronous REST fallback
loop, making one full tick exceed the health budget.

The follow-up production recurrence proved that quote REST fanout was only the
first layer. `_snapshot_freshness_observability()` still ran before tradeable
discovery and received the full snapshot candidate universe. With thousands of
candidates, it expanded per-symbol lifecycle/transfer freshness status for
observability even when all candidates were later blocked by strategy edge/window
rules and no entry data plane work was needed. That is the same V1 boundary
mistake in a different subsystem: observability for entry execution scope was
using full scan scope instead of primary+shadow tracked scope.

The third recurrence after `13ea417` showed the same boundary mistake in the
final entry freshness gate. Although the quote revalidator had only 16 tracked
quote targets and 4 REST resolutions, `_filter_candidates_by_snapshot_freshness()`
still iterated the full tradeable candidate set. That preserved fail-closed
safety, but it spent CPU and emitted per-candidate freshness decisions for
untracked candidates that V1 would never promote into execution-grade market
data.

The fourth recurrence after `a87c249` showed the same boundary mistake in the
warmup/fallback diagnostic path. `_snapshot_health_candidate_freshness_scope()`
limited its returned sample list to 48 rows, but it still iterated every
snapshot candidate and called `_candidate_snapshot_freshness_decisions()` before
the cap could help. Under `last_good_sidecar`, that happened before
primary+shadow tracking and before quote revalidation, so a diagnostic payload
alone could overrun the 60s full-tick budget.

### Fix

- Added `_select_v1_entry_tracked_scope()` as the shared V1 primary+shadow
  scope helper.
- The helper uses `select_tracked_opportunities()`, with primary count
  `min(entry_local_l2_primary_count, max_concurrent_positions)` and configured
  `shadow_entry_opportunity_count`, then returns candidates in the original
  ranking order.
- WS-BBO prewarm and `_entry_quote_revalidate_for_candidates()` now receive only
  that tracked candidate scope.
- Local-L2 activation keeps using the same tracked helper, so WS-BBO and
  Local-L2 data planes share the V1 execution-scope boundary.
- CL-071 behavior remains intact for tracked candidates: WS-BBO budget-excluded
  top candidate legs still continue into REST/top-book fallback, and missing
  REST capability remains an explicit fail-closed blocker.
- `last_scan` and `runtime.entry_quote_revalidate_probe` now expose
  `quote_revalidate_candidate_scope`, `quote_revalidate_candidate_count`,
  `quote_revalidate_all_target_count`, and
  `quote_revalidate_skipped_untracked_count`.
- Snapshot freshness observability now starts with global snapshot/quote health
  only (`snapshot_freshness_candidate_scope=global_snapshot`,
  `snapshot_freshness_candidate_count=0`) and refreshes candidate-scoped status
  only after V1 primary+shadow tracked candidates exist.
- Final `_filter_candidates_by_snapshot_freshness()` still evaluates real
  candidates before entry, but in WS-BBO/Local-L2 effective modes its input is
  now the V1 primary+shadow tracked candidates. Quote/freshness safety remains
  fail-closed for every candidate that can reach selection, while untracked
  shortlist rows no longer consume execution-grade freshness filtering.
- `last_scan` now records `snapshot_freshness_filter_candidate_scope`,
  `snapshot_freshness_filter_candidate_count`,
  `snapshot_freshness_filter_all_candidate_count`, and
  `snapshot_freshness_filter_skipped_untracked_count`.
- `runtime.snapshot_fallback_last_good` health payloads now build
  `candidate_freshness_scope` from the same V1 primary+shadow candidates in
  WS-BBO/Local-L2 effective modes, instead of evaluating the whole snapshot
  candidate universe before warmup completes.
- The fallback health payload now records
  `candidate_freshness_candidate_scope`,
  `candidate_freshness_candidate_count`,
  `candidate_freshness_all_candidate_count`, and
  `candidate_freshness_skipped_untracked_count`.

- No strategy, OI, liquidity, margin/admission, sizing, order submit/cancel,
  close, recovery, or residual-repair semantics changed.
- No production state mutation or live order operation is part of the local fix.

## Cluster CL-074: Runtime heartbeat health gate exporter-only false progress

### GitNexus Keys

- Fingerprint:
  `runtime-heartbeat-health-gate-exporter-only-false-progress`
- Components:
  `live-runtime`, `current-state`, `production-health`, `diagnose-live`,
  `production-observability`
- Symbols:
  `EngineState`, `LiveRuntime.run_loop`, `_current_state_heartbeat_loop`,
  `_export_current_state_snapshot`, `analyze_current_state`,
  `_build_state_consistency`, `_build_production_acceptance_gate`
- Watch:
  `live_tick_stale`, `exporter_only_progress`,
  `production_health.current_state.details.progress_source`,
  `runtime_progress.active_lane`,
  `runtime_progress.active_lane_overdue`,
  `runtime_market_data_config.entry_readiness_provider_effective`,
  `runtime_market_data_config.local_l2_effective_enabled`,
  `local_l2_residual_runtime_enabled`,
  `runtime.maker_event_no_ws_bbo_quote`

### Production Symptom

Several deployment reviews showed that a fresh
`runtime/live-state-current.json` could make the verifier look less severe even
when the actual live loop had not produced a recent scan or tick. That blurred
three different truths:

- the current-state exporter task is still writing a JSON file;
- the main runtime loop or a specific V1 lane is making business progress;
- exchange truth proves the account is flat and has no open orders.

The previous gate could treat fresh `generated_at_ms` / `current_state_age_ms`
as runtime progress. That was a surface fix: it could hide a stuck main loop as
long as the heartbeat exporter kept writing.

### Source Truth

- V1-compatible runtime health must be tied to business progress, not merely to
  file export freshness.
- Exchange truth remains the authority for flat/no-open-order acceptance, but
  flat truth alone does not prove the live loop is advancing.
- A bounded active lane is legitimate progress only while it is within its
  budget. Once `active_lane_age_ms > active_lane_budget_ms`, it must become a
  stale-loop signal again.

### Fix

- `EngineState` now carries optional `runtime_progress`.
- `LiveRuntime.run_loop()` records:
  `loop_iteration_started_ms`, `loop_iteration_completed_ms`,
  `last_lane_progress_ms`, `active_lane`, `active_lane_started_ms`,
  `active_lane_budget_ms`, and `active_lane_overdue`.
- Lane boundaries now cover the V1 runtime work lanes:
  `full_tick`, `active_positions`, `rate_limit_reload`, `local_l2_sync`,
  `passive_close`, `normal_exit`, `maker_event`,
  `pending_entry_maintenance`, and `housekeeping`.
- `_current_state_heartbeat_loop()` remains exporter-only. It does not advance
  `runtime_progress.last_lane_progress_ms`.
- Current-state export includes `runtime_progress` and derives
  `active_lane_overdue` from active lane age and budget.
- `analyze_current_state()` no longer uses `current_state_age_ms` to suppress
  `live_tick_stale`. Suppression now requires clean local state, flat exchange
  truth, and one of:
  recent `last_scan`, recent `runtime_progress.last_lane_progress_ms`, or a
  non-overdue bounded `active_lane`.
- A fresh exporter with stale business progress keeps `live_tick_stale` and
  adds `exporter_only_progress`; details expose `progress_source`,
  `exporter_only_progress`, and the normalized `runtime_progress`.
- `diagnose_live.py` exposes the same `runtime_progress` in both state
  consistency and production acceptance gate output.
- Follow-up root cause: cloud still had an untracked local `config/live.toml`
  with `local_l2_enabled=true` and no explicit
  `entry_readiness_provider="ws_bbo_quote_lease"`. Pull-only deploys cannot
  change that file, so the runtime itself now supplies effective live-provider
  semantics.
- Missing live `entry_readiness_provider` now defaults to
  `ws_bbo_quote_lease`; explicit `entry_readiness_provider="local_l2"` remains
  the required opt-in for V1 Local-L2 mode.
- WS BBO effective mode treats `local_l2_enabled=true` as configured legacy
  residual only. Local-L2 startup, dynamic L2 activation, snapshot sync,
  Local-L2 selection/final/execution gates, and Local-L2 readiness sessions are
  effective-disabled, and current state exports `runtime_market_data_config`.
- Pending passive maker-event maintenance in WS BBO mode uses fresh maker-venue
  BBO quotes from `ws_bbo_cache` and still calls the in-situ pending hedge
  driver. Missing/stale/invalid BBO fails closed with
  `runtime.maker_event_no_ws_bbo_quote` and does not fall back to Local-L2.
- `diagnose_live.py` adds `local_l2_residual_runtime_enabled`: in WS BBO
  effective mode, any `runtime.local_l2_*` or Local-L2 selection/readiness
  event is a blocking regression.
- Final follow-up: WS BBO effective mode also clears Local-L2 runtime
  containers and persisted `retained_local_l2_books`,
  `local_l2_books_snapshot`, and `local_l2_session_snapshot` during startup
  restore and runtime snapshot persistence. This closes the case where the
  active Local-L2 worker/event plane was off but old persisted book snapshots
  could remain in `runtime/live-state.json` until a new runtime snapshot wrote
  them away.

## Cluster CL-073: SAHARA entry residual dust 2% warning split

### GitNexus Keys

- Fingerprint:
  `sahara-entry-residual-dust-two-percent-diagnose-warning-split`
- Components:
  `live-runtime`, `residual-repair`, `diagnose-live`, `pending-entry`,
  `v1-parity`, `exchange-docs`
- Symbols:
  `_terminalize_residual_repair_task`,
  `_recover_residual_repairs`,
  `_build_entry_quantity_terminal_summary`
- Watch:
  `execution.entry_residual_dust_tolerated`,
  `pending_entry.hedge_quantity_undercut`,
  `execution.entry_quantity_plan`,
  `hedge_quantity_undercut_warning_count`,
  `common_quantity_mismatch_warning_count`,
  `account_wallet_signer_mismatch`

### Production Symptom

Cloud read-only review of deploy `6b92cd1` found the current runtime healthy and
flat: no open positions, pending entries, pending closes, passive closes,
residual repairs, or venue open orders. One post-deploy `SAHARAUSDT` lifecycle
opened and closed successfully. During that lifecycle, the maker leg filled
larger than the normalized OKX hedge quantity, then a residual repair closed the
remaining `11` units.

The event shape looked like a short single-leg window, but the residual was
about `11 / 2860 ~= 0.38%`. The desired V1-compatible acceptance rule is that
this is not an abnormal warehouse condition when exchange quantity rules prove
the remainder cannot form a valid order.

### Source Truth

- V1 processes pending entry hedges, passive closes, and residual repairs as
  separate lifecycle work. Residual repair remains the authority for live
  excess, but unrepairable exchange-min dust may terminalize.
- Bybit V5 open/closed order docs warn that recent closed-status visibility can
  be bounded and latency/restart-sensitive, so an early missing order query is
  not terminal proof by itself.
- OKX SWAP/FUTURES sizing depends on contract rules (`ctVal`, `lotSz`) and
  order/amend truth must retain live order identity when amend fails but the
  original order is still live.

### Fix

- Entry residual dust tolerance is tightened from `<=5%` to `<=2%`.
- The runtime still requires exchange-min terminal evidence:
  `exchange_min_quantity_dust` or `exchange_min_notional_dust`. A ratio alone
  cannot mark a residual tolerated.
- Exchange-min entry residuals above `2%` are paused with
  `entry_residual_dust_over_tolerance` and remain pending; the pair gate is not
  released.
- Repairable residuals still submit reduce-only repair, compensation, or block;
  missing exchange rule evidence does not get the tolerated-dust fast path.
- Diagnose now reports both raw quantity events and warning-only events:
  tolerated `<=2%` exchange-min dust entries are excluded from
  `hedge_quantity_undercut_warning_count` and
  `common_quantity_mismatch_warning_count`.
- Production acceptance now also exposes `short_window_warning_count`,
  `short_window_warning_families`, and `short_window_warning_details`, so a
  final flat state cannot hide entry quantity mismatch, hedge undercut,
  passive-close truth-gap, or passive zero-fill recovered windows.
- Hyperliquid `account_wallet_signer_mismatch` is tracked separately as a
  preflight/env identity issue. It is not classified as a SAHARA open/close
  residual bug.
