### Production Symptom

A historical review of 70 non-normal positions showed the exchange exposure
was not the primary failure: 67 positions had complete real exchange open/close
lifecycle evidence but project lifecycle/accounting records were incomplete,
while 3 positions were `entry.opened` records with zero matched quantity and no
real exchange fill. Confirmed exchange-bad/unclosed real positions in this set:
0.

The same bug family made optimization samples unsafe. Local project records
could treat legacy `exit.closed`, incomplete `exit.reconciled`, or local
`net_quote` as normal PnL. That allowed TAIKO-style fake positive PnL to enter
analysis without complete exchange fill/fee/funding evidence.

### Root Cause

V2 had several independent lifecycle truths:

- runtime `entry.opened`, `exit.closed`, `exit.reconciled`, `order.filled`,
  passive-close truth gaps, terminal-flat cleanup, and offline optimization
  each reconstructed normality differently.
- close accounting backfill was candidate/zero-leg oriented instead of
  target-quantity coverage oriented, so partial shortfall cases such as
  `15/24 + later 9` could remain misclassified.
- Bitget raw trade history needed venue-normalized `tradeSide=close` semantics;
  relying on raw `side` or stale candidates could miss the real close leg.
- `entry.opened matched_quantity=0` was allowed to look like a position record
  instead of being classified as a phantom/terminal zero-fill open.

### V1 / Exchange Decision

- Exchange truth outranks project lifecycle records.
- A normal optimization sample requires complete exchange lifecycle truth and
  PnL evidence refs from real fill/order/funding facts.
- `exit.closed` is not proof of normality without exchange fill coverage.
- A close leg is complete only when accumulated fills cover the expected target
  quantity for that leg.
- Zero-quantity `entry.opened` is never a normal position sample.

### Fix

- Added `lightfee/lifecycle/exchange_truth_ledger.py`, a read-only canonical
  lifecycle ledger that emits `exchange_lifecycle_complete`,
  `phantom_zero_qty_opened`, `exchange_lifecycle_incomplete`, or
  `evidence_incomplete` per position.
- The ledger aggregates open and close fill coverage by target quantity per
  long/short leg, records order identity evidence, computes exchange-derived
  PnL, and maps Bitget `tradeSide=close` through venue/leg ownership.
- Fill evidence is de-duplicated across local `order.filled`,
  `exit.reconciled`, raw trade history, and rebuild query sources so a later
  truth probe cannot double-count an already-accounted fill.
- PnL now uses exchange open fills plus exchange close fills, signed fee
  statements, de-duplicated funding statements, `net_pnl_bps`, and evidence
  refs. Local `entry.opened` prices or local `net_quote` are not accepted as
  profit truth.
- Added `scripts/rebuild_lifecycle_truth.py` for dry-run historical rebuilds
  and append-only `accounting.lifecycle_truth_rebuilt` correction events.
- Historical `--apply` is fail-closed on missing position scope, query errors,
  incomplete/evidence-incomplete lifecycle, missing PnL refs, or expected-count
  mismatches.
- `trade_optimization.py` now consumes lifecycle truth and excludes samples
  whose lifecycle is incomplete or whose PnL lacks exchange evidence refs.
- Existing close backfill work keeps accounting-only terminal-flat gaps
  nonblocking for trading while retaining statement probes for later
  accounting completion.
