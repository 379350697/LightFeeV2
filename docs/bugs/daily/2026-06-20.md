# 2026-06-20

## Cluster CL-103 - Passive close final truth single-leg cleanup contract

| Status | Severity | Family | Modules | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| runtime fix `fb1c3a0` deployed; cloud gate green with exchange flat/no-open-orders, `risk_mode=running`, no unmapped events | critical | passive close terminal truth + risk-only containment | business-contract, passive-close, unpaired-recovery, diagnose-live, lifecycle | `lightfee/engine/business_contract.py`, `lightfee/engine/passive_close.py`, `lightfee/engine/unpaired_live_position_recovery.py`, `scripts/diagnose_live.py`, `lightfee/engine/v1_lifecycle_closure.py` |

### Fingerprint

`truth_available=true; open_orders_flat=true; one live leg nonzero; passive close repeats retain_pending/retry_exchange_position_open_order_truth; risk_only/fail_closed looks like terminal handling`

### Evidence

- Cloud ESPORTSUSDT logs showed final passive-close truth was trusted: Bybit
  quantity `0`, Binance quantity `470`, and both venues had no open orders.
  The runtime still emitted `exit.passive_close_waiting_exchange_flat_truth`
  with `decision=retain_pending` and
  `next_action=retry_exchange_position_open_order_truth`.
- The expected business terminal state had already been documented: cleanup is
  complete only when exchange positions are flat, open orders are flat, and the
  local owner has been removed. `risk_only` and `fail_closed` are containment
  states, not business terminal states.
- Existing unpaired recovery guards were intentionally conservative for
  ownerless live positions, especially when automatic recovery is disabled or
  the notional cap is exceeded. That diagnostic-only path must not become the
  fallback for an owned pending passive close.
- First cloud deploy of the owned one-sided cleanup cleared the actual ESPORTS
  exchange truth and local owners, but diagnose still showed two non-green
  tails: persisted `risk_mode=fail_closed` after cleanup and unmapped
  `runtime.passive_close_recovery_result`. That proved the missing piece was
  not order cleanup anymore; it was terminal-state/lifecycle cleanup after the
  business owner had already reached exchange flat/no-open-orders.

### Root Cause

- `_finalize_passive_close()` consumed final live truth only as
  `flat -> clear` or `not flat -> retain_pending`. It did not classify the
  trusted single-leg residual shape as actionable owned close work.
- The one-sided reduce-only IOC flatten path already existed, including
  open-order-flat proof and post-fill live-flat proof, but final passive-close
  truth did not call it.
- `business_contract.py` centralized diagnostic vocabulary but did not yet
  provide a final passive-close action contract for runtime.
- Diagnose could show a waiting passive close, but did not surface that the
  waiting event was actually actionable as `flatten_remaining_live_leg`.
- Unpaired live-position skip/fail events did not explicitly say they were
  current high-risk exposure and non-terminal business states.
- The stale fail-closed cleanup contract existed, but passive-close owner
  cleanup did not call it after removing the last open/pending owner. It was
  effectively waiting for another runtime housekeeping/startup pass, so the
  cleared business could still diagnose as `risk_mode_fail_closed`.
- `runtime.passive_close_recovery_result` had already been recorded as a
  non-blocking CL-101 follow-up. Because it was not classified by the business
  contract, `diagnose_live.py --since-deploy` still exposed it as unmapped.
- `risk_only_live_single_leg_exposure_count` was initially event-counted from
  since-deploy history. That made recovered `auto_disabled`/risk-only evidence
  look like current exposure after current unpaired records were already empty.

### Fix

- Added pure `passive_close_final_truth_contract(...)`:
  `clear_flat`, `flatten_remaining_live_leg`,
  `adopt_or_block_existing_close_order`, `retain_untrusted_truth`, and
  `fail_closed_manual_block`.
- `_finalize_passive_close()` now consumes that contract. Trusted one-sided
  residual plus flat open-order truth calls the existing owned
  `_flatten_live_one_sided_position()` path. If the immediate follow-up live
  snapshot is already zero, runtime still calls `_clear_if_live_flat()` before
  removing local state, so local cleanup cannot bypass fresh exchange
  flat/no-open-orders proof.
- Waiting events now carry `passive_close_final_truth_action`,
  `business_contract_action`, `diagnostic_severity`, and a contract reason
  instead of always saying `retry_exchange_position_open_order_truth`.
- Unpaired recovery skip/fail events now expose `current_risk_exposure`,
  `business_terminal=false`, `diagnostic_severity`, and `next_action`.
  Ownerless `auto_disabled` and `cap_exceeded` remain no-auto-order paths, but
  they are diagnosed as current exposure instead of terminal cleanup.
- Diagnose now reports
  `passive_close_actionable_single_leg_wait_count`,
  `passive_close_final_truth_actions`, and
  `risk_only_live_single_leg_exposure_count`; unpaired recovery details also
  show `current_risk_exposure` and `business_terminal`.
- Lifecycle mapping continues through the business contract / passive-close
  prefix so `exit.passive_close_live_one_sided_flatten` is V1
  `PASSIVE_CLOSE`, not an unmapped runtime event.
- Terminal live-flat passive-close cleanup now calls
  `clear_stale_fail_closed_if_recovery_clean(...)` after owner removal and V1
  recovery-core evidence. Operator-requested fail-closed, pending work, live
  unpaired recovery, and recovery block reasons still prevent clearing.
- `classify_business_event_kind(...)` now classifies
  `runtime.passive_close_recovery_result` as V1 `PASSIVE_CLOSE` evidence, so
  lifecycle closure consumes it through the unified business contract instead
  of a diagnose-local patch.
- `diagnose_live.py` overrides
  `risk_only_live_single_leg_exposure_count` from current unpaired recovery
  records. Historical risk-only events remain in `event_counts` and journals,
  but no longer masquerade as current exposure after terminal flat cleanup.

### Non-goals

  acceptable final business cleanup states while exchange truth still shows a
  live position.
