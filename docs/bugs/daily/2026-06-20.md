## Cluster CL-104 - Quote/OI entry admission evidence and reconciliation accounting gap contract

| Status | Severity | Family | Modules | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| runtime fix `447218a` deployed; final diagnostic-noise fix `091ce2c` deployed/cloud verified; post-deploy strict-review semantic follow-up retired into later diagnostic-visibility clusters, no active deploy-pending item retained | high | entry admission evidence + close accounting evidence + diagnostic noise visibility | business-contract, diagnose-live, lifecycle, production health | `lightfee/engine/business_contract.py`, `scripts/diagnose_live.py`, `lightfee/engine/v1_lifecycle_closure.py`, `scripts/verify_production_services.py` |

### Fingerprint

`quote_rewarm over-budget/stale evidence; OI targeted refresh resolved/failed counters; exit.reconciled evidence_gap=true after exchange flat/no-open-orders; missing_short_close_trade_statement`

### Evidence

- Latest cloud review still had the important safety baseline:
  `gate_passed=true`, exchange positions flat, no exchange open orders, no
  local open/pending owner, no unmapped lifecycle event, and no blank quote
  rewarm action.
- The noisy tail was not current exposure. Quote stale / quote rewarm / OI
  targeted refresh events describe whether a candidate has enough market
  evidence to open. They do not change close terminal truth and they must not
  loosen stale-quote, OI, liquidity, or entry admission gates.
- `HUSDT`-style `exit.reconciled evidence_gap=true` with
  `missing_short_close_trade_statement` is an accounting evidence gap after
  terminal exchange truth. It is not proof that the business failed to clear
  when exchange position truth and open-order truth are both clean.
- Official venue semantics reinforce the split: Binance `bookTicker` and
  `openInterest` are market/OI evidence sources; Binance `userTrades` and
  Bybit `/v5/execution/list` are trade-statement/accounting evidence sources.
  Query windows, sorting, and multi-fill limits in statement APIs cannot
  replace live position and open-order truth.

### Root Cause

- `business_contract.py` already centralized several runtime/diagnostic
  classifications, but quote/OI admission evidence and reconciliation
  statement gaps still had local interpretations in diagnose paths.
- Diagnose could look at raw quote/OI counters and raw reconciliation gaps
  without first asking a shared contract whether the item was a current entry
  blocker, a recovered diagnostic artifact, a terminal candidate stale branch,
  or a non-blocking accounting gap after terminal flat truth.
- The earlier close terminal rule was correct, but the presentation layer did
  not explicitly state that statement/trade probe gaps are historical
  accounting evidence once the business owner is clear and exchange truth is
  flat/no-open-orders.
- After the cloud gate stayed green and real close terminal times were seconds,
  the remaining operator pain was presentation noise: resolved close artifacts,
  terminalized over-budget phases, and quote/OI candidate blockers appeared in
  adjacent summaries without one shared visibility vocabulary.
- A later strict review of the latest deployed window found no position/open-order
  risk regression, but it did expose three semantic tails that were still not
  unified enough: `exit.compensation_already_flat` could appear as an unmapped V1
  lifecycle event, non-handoff soft/hard over-budget phases could still lack
  explicit action evidence, and quote/OI unresolved blocker samples did not carry
  an explicit `entry_candidate_admission` scope. These were diagnostic contract
  gaps, not grounds to loosen quote/OI admission or close terminal truth.

### Fix

- Added pure `entry_market_evidence_contract(kind, payload)`:
  `allow_entry_evidence`, `block_stale_quote`, `block_oi_unavailable`,
  `terminal_candidate_rewarm`, `refresh_evidence`, and
  `diagnostic_recovered_overbudget`.
- Added pure `close_reconciliation_evidence_contract(payload,
  current_exchange_truth_clean)`: clean exchange truth classifies
  `exit.reconciled evidence_gap=true` as `terminal_flat_accounting_gap`; unclean
  or unavailable truth classifies it as `unresolved_close_accounting_gap`.
- `classify_business_event_kind(...)` now consumes both contracts before
  returning phase, terminality, action, evidence class, blocking status, and
  diagnostic severity.
- `diagnose_live.py` now exposes root-level
  `entry_market_evidence_summary` and
  `close_reconciliation_evidence_gap_summary`. Quote/OI blocker counts are
  contract-derived, and reconciliation gaps are separated into terminal
  accounting evidence versus unresolved close accounting blockers.
- Lifecycle remains business-contract driven. No new event kind is allowed to
  become an unmapped V1 lifecycle drift just because the diagnostic vocabulary
  was centralized.
- Follow-up diagnostic-noise contract adds
  `classify_noise_visibility(kind, payload, current_exchange_truth_clean=...)`
  and root `diagnostic_noise_summary`. Current exposure remains
  `current_blocker`; quote/OI candidate evidence is
  `current_admission_blocker`; resolved close artifacts and terminalized
  over-budget phases become `historical_terminal_evidence`.
- `short_window_warning_details.passive_close_truth_gap` now counts only
  unresolved/current order-truth gaps. Resolved ACK-only / duplicate-client /
  reduce-only / post-only close artifacts stay visible in resolved/historical
  summaries, but no longer look like current short-window warnings after clean
  exchange truth.
- Strict follow-up review closed three diagnostic-noise accounting gaps:
  resolved close artifacts are not double-counted, flat/no-open-orders
  reconciliation gaps stay historical even when another gate blocker exists,
  and current risk-only/single-leg exposure from the recovery summary is counted
  as a current blocker even without a matching raw cleanup event in the window.
- Bug-ledger governance follow-up on 2026-07-02 removed the stale
  `deploy pending` label for this historical follow-up. The production baseline
  remains `447218a` + `091ce2c`; later CL-105 and CL-143 entries carry the
  subsequent deployed diagnostic-visibility proof.
- Final cloud review of `586a7b8` caught one remaining presentation bug:
  historical `recovery.unpaired_live_position_cleanup_skipped` events from the
  since-deploy window were still classified as `current_blocker` even after
  current exchange truth was flat/no-open-orders and
  `unpaired_live_position_recovery_summary.current_risk_exposure_count=0`.
  The contract now downgrades those events to
  `historical_terminal_evidence` when current exchange truth is clean. A true
  current single-leg/risk-only exposure still requires unclean exchange truth or
  a current recovery-summary exposure count.
- Post-deploy strict-review follow-up keeps the same unified vocabulary:
  `exit.compensation_already_flat` now maps through
  `classify_business_event_kind(...)` as V1 `PASSIVE_CLOSE`,
  `terminal_flat_already_proven`, with action
  `record_compensation_terminal_flat`. Phase-duration fallback now fills
  configured action/evidence for action-required non-handoff phases while leaving
  `candidate_lease` as handoff-quality evidence. Quote/OI samples and unresolved
  blocker summaries now expose `scope=entry_candidate_admission` so admission
  noise is not confused with current live exposure; synthetic risk-only live
  exposure samples use `scope=current_live_exposure` so diagnostic sample schemas
  stay explicit even when the current exposure count comes from the business
  progression summary rather than a raw journal event.

## Cluster CL-103 - Passive close final truth single-leg cleanup contract

| Status | Severity | Family | Modules | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| runtime fix `fb1c3a0` deployed; cloud gate green with exchange flat/no-open-orders, `risk_mode=running`, no unmapped events | critical | passive close terminal truth + risk-only containment | business-contract, passive-close, unpaired-recovery, diagnose-live, lifecycle | `lightfee/engine/business_contract.py`, `lightfee/engine/passive_close.py`, `lightfee/engine/unpaired_live_position_recovery.py`, `scripts/diagnose_live.py`, `lightfee/engine/v1_lifecycle_closure.py` |

### Fingerprint

`truth_available=true; open_orders_flat=true; one live leg nonzero; passive close repeats retain_pending/retry_exchange_position_open_order_truth; risk_only/fail_closed looks like terminal handling`

### Evidence

- Cloud ESPORTSUSDT logs showed final passive-close truth was trusted: Bybit
  quantity `0`, Binance quantity `470`, and both venues had no open orders.
  The runtime still emitted `exit.passive_close_waiting_exchange_flat_truth`
  with `decision=retain_pending` and
  `next_action=retry_exchange_position_open_order_truth`.
- The expected business terminal state had already been documented: cleanup is
  complete only when exchange positions are flat, open orders are flat, and the
  local owner has been removed. `risk_only` and `fail_closed` are containment
  states, not business terminal states.
- Existing unpaired recovery guards were intentionally conservative for
  ownerless live positions, especially when automatic recovery is disabled or
  the notional cap is exceeded. That diagnostic-only path must not become the
  fallback for an owned pending passive close.
- First cloud deploy of the owned one-sided cleanup cleared the actual ESPORTS
  exchange truth and local owners, but diagnose still showed two non-green
  tails: persisted `risk_mode=fail_closed` after cleanup and unmapped
  `runtime.passive_close_recovery_result`. That proved the missing piece was
  not order cleanup anymore; it was terminal-state/lifecycle cleanup after the
  business owner had already reached exchange flat/no-open-orders.

### Root Cause

- `_finalize_passive_close()` consumed final live truth only as
  `flat -> clear` or `not flat -> retain_pending`. It did not classify the
  trusted single-leg residual shape as actionable owned close work.
- The one-sided reduce-only IOC flatten path already existed, including
  open-order-flat proof and post-fill live-flat proof, but final passive-close
  truth did not call it.
- `business_contract.py` centralized diagnostic vocabulary but did not yet
  provide a final passive-close action contract for runtime.
- Diagnose could show a waiting passive close, but did not surface that the
  waiting event was actually actionable as `flatten_remaining_live_leg`.
- Unpaired live-position skip/fail events did not explicitly say they were
  current high-risk exposure and non-terminal business states.
- The stale fail-closed cleanup contract existed, but passive-close owner
  cleanup did not call it after removing the last open/pending owner. It was
  effectively waiting for another runtime housekeeping/startup pass, so the
  cleared business could still diagnose as `risk_mode_fail_closed`.
- `runtime.passive_close_recovery_result` had already been recorded as a
  non-blocking CL-101 follow-up. Because it was not classified by the business
  contract, `diagnose_live.py --since-deploy` still exposed it as unmapped.
- `risk_only_live_single_leg_exposure_count` was initially event-counted from
  since-deploy history. That made recovered `auto_disabled`/risk-only evidence
  look like current exposure after current unpaired records were already empty.

### Fix

- Added pure `passive_close_final_truth_contract(...)`:
  `clear_flat`, `flatten_remaining_live_leg`,
  `adopt_or_block_existing_close_order`, `retain_untrusted_truth`, and
  `fail_closed_manual_block`.
- `_finalize_passive_close()` now consumes that contract. Trusted one-sided
  residual plus flat open-order truth calls the existing owned
  `_flatten_live_one_sided_position()` path. If the immediate follow-up live
  snapshot is already zero, runtime still calls `_clear_if_live_flat()` before
  removing local state, so local cleanup cannot bypass fresh exchange
  flat/no-open-orders proof.
- Waiting events now carry `passive_close_final_truth_action`,
  `business_contract_action`, `diagnostic_severity`, and a contract reason
  instead of always saying `retry_exchange_position_open_order_truth`.
- Unpaired recovery skip/fail events now expose `current_risk_exposure`,
  `business_terminal=false`, `diagnostic_severity`, and `next_action`.
  Ownerless `auto_disabled` and `cap_exceeded` remain no-auto-order paths, but
  they are diagnosed as current exposure instead of terminal cleanup.
- Diagnose now reports
  `passive_close_actionable_single_leg_wait_count`,
  `passive_close_final_truth_actions`, and
  `risk_only_live_single_leg_exposure_count`; unpaired recovery details also
  show `current_risk_exposure` and `business_terminal`.
- Lifecycle mapping continues through the business contract / passive-close
  prefix so `exit.passive_close_live_one_sided_flatten` is V1
  `PASSIVE_CLOSE`, not an unmapped runtime event.
- Terminal live-flat passive-close cleanup now calls
  `clear_stale_fail_closed_if_recovery_clean(...)` after owner removal and V1
  recovery-core evidence. Operator-requested fail-closed, pending work, live
  unpaired recovery, and recovery block reasons still prevent clearing.
- `classify_business_event_kind(...)` now classifies
  `runtime.passive_close_recovery_result` as V1 `PASSIVE_CLOSE` evidence, so
  lifecycle closure consumes it through the unified business contract instead
  of a diagnose-local patch.
- `diagnose_live.py` overrides
  `risk_only_live_single_leg_exposure_count` from current unpaired recovery
  records. Historical risk-only events remain in `event_counts` and journals,
  but no longer masquerade as current exposure after terminal flat cleanup.
