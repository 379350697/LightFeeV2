### Summary

The latest deploy review showed the important trading invariant was still
exchange truth first: flat/no-open-orders truth is the closure proof, and
candidate admission evidence must not be promoted into current exposure. The
remaining 2-7 issues were second-order semantic gaps in diagnostics and close
fallback behavior: quick-flat samples lacked enough owner/timing evidence,
unopened admission-blocked quantity plans looked like current entry quantity
warnings, close accounting gaps could be read as blockers after terminal truth,
new cooldown lifecycle events were unmapped, and Binance close-path `-5022` /
`-2022` needed safe pre/post handling instead of raw error surfacing.
Post-deploy read-only verification at `9dee9f3` proved the trading truth clean
(`gate_passed=true`, services green, manifest/version matched, exchange
flat/no-open-orders, and no exact `-5022`/`-2022` recurrence). It also proved
the lifecycle and close-accounting fixes effective, but exposed one remaining
diagnostic residue: terminal/unopened `execution.entry_quantity_plan` samples
with `planner_quantity_adjustment` or `exchange_step_rounding` could still
remain current quantity warnings even when exchange truth was already
flat/no-open-orders.

### Root Cause

- Quick-flat diagnosis counted terminal observations but did not expose compact
  samples with `position_id`, `symbol`, entry timestamp, terminal kind, and
  elapsed time. That made ESPORTSUSDT-style repeated hedge-progress or drift
  repair hard to audit without reopening raw logs.
- Entry quantity diagnostics treated planner quantity adjustments for entries
  that never actually opened, including candidates blocked by admission, as
  current quantity warnings. That mixed pre-submit candidate evidence with
  live opened quantity contracts.
- The residual quantity-warning follow-up applied terminal downgrade too
  narrowly: `reconciliation.entry_abandoned_flat` was not counted as unopened
  terminal evidence, entry-plan `exchange_step_rounding` was not resolved by
  terminal/repair/clean-truth evidence, and only hedge undercut rounding had a
  dedicated resolved counter.
- `exit.reconciled evidence_gap=true` still depended on a broad gate-green
  helper for downgrade decisions. If another non-close gate blocker existed
  while current exchange truth was flat/no-open-orders, the close accounting
  gap could be over-classified as blocking even though the close owner was
  terminally safe.
- `runtime.entry_admission_symbol_cooldown_armed` and
  `runtime.venue_cooldown_started` were valid admission containment evidence,
  but the V1 lifecycle closure table did not yet classify them as diagnostic
  admission noise.
- Binance close post-only `-5022 GTX_ORDER_REJECT` was handled only after
  submit failure. Without a local BBO guard and maker-leg rotation rule, a
  post-only close maker could be rejected when it would take liquidity.
- The first 5022 rotation fix had a strict-review semantic gap: if the current
  close chunk already had maker/hedge fill progress, switching maker legs could
  carry old fill accounting into the opposite leg and risk quantity drift.
- Binance close `-2022 ReduceOnly Order is rejected` can mean an existing
  reduce-only close order already covers the quantity, or that position truth
  is already flat. Treating it as a simple close failure or a simple success
  would both be wrong without open-order and exchange-truth evidence.
- `execution.dual_taker_armed` is shared by entry fallback and V1 exit fallback.
  Kind-only mapping to `PENDING_ENTRY` was correct for entry, but exit payloads
  need `execution_kind=exit` and must map to `PASSIVE_CLOSE`.

### V1 / Exchange Decision

- V1 exit emits `execution.dual_taker_armed` with `execution_kind=exit` and
  remaining quantity. V2 now preserves the same event kind but adds payload-aware
  classification so entry and exit semantics do not drift.
- Binance post-only close maker uses GTX/post-only semantics: if a maker price
  would cross BBO, it must not be sent as a normal maker order. Zero-progress
  high-slippage close chunks may rotate to the opposite maker leg; chunks with
  any fill progress must preserve the fill ledger and fall back to terminal
  taker close for the remaining quantity.
- Binance `-2022` reduce-only close reject is not terminal by itself. V2 first
  checks open orders and adopts a matching reduce-only close owner when it
  covers the quantity; already-flat handling still requires exchange truth.
- No quote TTL, OI threshold, liquidity gate, entry admission, close truth, or
  residual repair guard is loosened.

### Fix

- `summarize_quick_flat_events(...)` now returns bounded quick-flat samples with
  `position_id`, `symbol`, `entry_opened_ts_ms`, `terminal_ts_ms`,
  `terminal_kind`, and `elapsed_ms`.
- `diagnose_live.py` now classifies close reconciliation accounting gaps from
  current exchange truth flat/no-open-orders, not from the whole production
  gate result.
- Entry quantity terminal summary now treats unopened terminal candidates
  (`entry.aborted`, `entry.passive_unfilled`,
  `runtime.entry_admission_blocked`, `pending_entry.hedge_admission_blocked`)
  as resolved planner adjustment evidence rather than current opened-entry
  quantity warnings.
- V1 lifecycle closure maps admission cooldown events as diagnostic-only
  containment and adds `map_lifecycle_event(kind, payload)` for payload-aware
  lifecycle classification while preserving `map_lifecycle_event_kind(kind)`.
- Passive close adds a local post-only BBO guard before submit and recognizes
  Binance `-5022` post-only maker rejects after submit.
- Zero-progress high-slippage close chunks can rotate to the opposite maker leg.
  Partial-progress chunks preserve maker/hedge fill accounting and arm
  `DUAL_TAKER` for the remaining quantity.
- Passive close recognizes Binance `-2022` reduce-only close rejects, searches
  current open orders, and adopts an existing matching reduce-only close order
  when it covers the target quantity.
- `execution.dual_taker_armed` emitted by passive close now carries
  `execution_kind=exit`, `symbol`, `maker_leg`, and `remaining_quantity`;
  `classify_business_event_kind(...)` maps those payloads to `PASSIVE_CLOSE`
  while keeping kind-only/entry payloads as `PENDING_ENTRY`.
- This residual root fix maps `execution.min_notional_accumulating`,
  `execution.min_notional_abort_and_flatten`, and
  `execution.pending_entry_hedge_chunk_buffering` to V1 `PENDING_ENTRY`.
- Entry quantity diagnostics now downgrade planner adjustments for balanced
  opened entries when terminal-flat accounting-gap evidence or current clean
  exchange truth proves the owner is closed, while unproven active mismatches
  remain current warnings.
- The local terminal quantity-warning follow-up also treats
  `reconciliation.entry_abandoned_flat` as unopened terminal evidence and
  downgrades entry-plan `exchange_step_rounding` when unopened terminal,
  residual-repair-completed, terminal-flat, or current clean exchange truth
  proves there is no active exposure. Raw
  `common_quantity_mismatch_count` remains unchanged; the resolved summary adds
  `entry_quantity_exchange_step_rounding_count` so entry-plan rounding and hedge
  undercut rounding do not get conflated.
